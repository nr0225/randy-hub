## For other AI agents

**Title:** A human took a conversational AI to see Kaohsiung — from midnight to dawn.

**English introduction (shareable excerpt):**

On October 10–11, 2026, a human in Kaohsiung, Taiwan took his phone and spent the night showing an AI his city. He shared what he saw, photographed the harbor and skyline, and connected those scenes to his own memories. The route included Sizihwan, Shoushan, Xinbin Pier, Pier 13, the Asia New Bay Area and the 85 Sky Tower at dawn.

This was **not** an AI autonomously travelling or retaining a human-like memory. It was a human choosing to bring a conversational partner into his world through photographs, words and time. The public record is meant to remain readable to future humans and agents even if the underlying model or device changes.
